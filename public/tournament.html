Tournament Overview

GT TOURNAMENT — SEASON 01 is a competitive Free Fire Battle Royale squad tournament organized by MKX ESPORTS.


Each registered squad consists of four players. Teams must register using their correct in-game details and follow all tournament rules.

Tournament Rules

  • BR Squad format — 4 players per team.
  • Only registered players are allowed to play.
  • Player changes require admin approval.
  • Hacks, scripts, mods and cheats are strictly prohibited.
  • Intentional glitches or exploits are not allowed.
  • Teams must follow room joining instructions.
  • Players must maintain respectful behaviour.
  • Valid screenshot/video evidence may be required for disputes.
  • Final decisions will be made by MKX ESPORTS Admin Team.

Important Information

Registration details must be entered correctly. Your Registration ID will be generated after successful submission. Keep your Registration ID safe for checking your registration status.
